Cookies Policy

This policy explains how Heritage Modern Spaces uses cookies and similar technologies, what they do, and how you can control them. We keep our cookie use minimal and respectful.

Last updated: 1 January 2026

Minimal cookies

We only use a small number of cookies — most are essential for the site to function properly.

No tracking for ads

We do not use cookies for advertising, profiling, or selling your data to third parties.

You're in control

You can block or delete cookies anytime through your browser settings. It's your choice.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or phone) when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, keep you logged in, and understand how visitors use the site.

Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, and pixel tags. For simplicity, we refer to all of these as "cookies" in this policy.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for a few limited purposes:

  • Essential functionality: To make the site work properly — for example, remembering your cookie preferences.
  • Analytics: To collect anonymous, aggregated data about how visitors use our site, so we can improve content and navigation.
  • Preferences: To remember small choices you make, such as dismissing a notice.

We do not use cookies for advertising, retargeting, or cross-site tracking. We do not sell or share cookie data with advertisers or data brokers.

Our approach: We believe cookies should serve you, not the other way around. We keep them to a minimum and only use them where they genuinely improve your experience.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Below is a detailed list of the cookies you may encounter on our site:

Cookie name Category Purpose Duration
hms_consent Essential Stores your cookie consent choices so we don't ask you repeatedly. 12 months
hms_session Essential Maintains basic session state for site functionality. Session
_ga Analytics Used by Google Analytics to distinguish unique visitors (anonymized IP). 24 months
_ga_* Analytics Used by Google Analytics to maintain session state. 24 months
hms_prefs Preferences Remembers small display preferences (e.g., dismissed notices). 6 months

Note: Cookie names and durations may change slightly as we update our site. We will update this table when they do.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services we use:

  • Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand site traffic and behavior. The data is anonymized and aggregated. You can learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and opt out via Google Analytics Opt-out.
  • Email delivery: If you subscribe to our newsletter, our email service provider may use tracking pixels to measure open rates. You can disable image loading in your email client to prevent this.

We do not control third-party cookies. Please refer to the respective providers' policies for more information.

5. How to Control Cookies

You have several options for managing or disabling cookies:

  • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Look for "Settings", "Preferences", or "Privacy" in your browser menu.
  • Cookie consent: When you first visit our site, you can choose which non-essential cookies to accept via our consent banner.
  • Opt-out tools: For analytics, you can use the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Please note that blocking essential cookies may affect how the site functions. Disabling analytics cookies will not affect your experience — it just means we won't see your visit in our anonymous statistics.

Browser-specific instructions

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies

6. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature. Because there is no industry consensus on how to interpret DNT signals, our site does not currently respond to them. However, we do not engage in cross-site tracking, so DNT signals have limited relevance here.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, regulation, or our practices. When we make significant changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
